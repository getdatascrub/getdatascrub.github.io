How portable mode works

Open the launcher, then open the add-in in Excel.

  1. Save the EXE somewhere you can find it, such as Downloads or Desktop
  2. Double-click it and choose Open in Excel
  3. The launcher detects your Excel architecture automatically
  4. Open or create a workbook and use the new ribbon tab
  5. Close Excel when finished; the add-in is not permanently installed

The portable launcher is the customer entry point.

Early-access Windows notice

The current trial launchers are awaiting commercial publisher signing. Windows or your organization’s security policy may identify them as unrecognized. If company policy blocks unsigned software, do not bypass that policy—ask your IT team to review the file or wait for the signed release. Downloading from this website improves the handoff but does not override Windows security controls.

Verify the source

Download only from getdatascrub.com and compare the SHA-256 value shown on this page after the final package is posted.

Your data stays in Excel

Workbook contents are processed locally. Online requests are limited to trial, licensing, and update status.

Trial timing

System requirements

Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021, 2024, or Microsoft 365 desktop for Windows. Both 32-bit and 64-bit Excel are supported. Excel for Mac, Excel Online, Google Sheets, and mobile Excel are not supported.
